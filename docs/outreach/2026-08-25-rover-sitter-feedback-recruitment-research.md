# Rover sitter feedback recruitment research

Research date: 2026-08-25  
Scope: Chicago northwest suburbs  
Status: research only; no outreach sent

## Recommendation

Do **not** recruit through Rover messages, booking requests, Meet & Greets, or other Rover interactions. Rover's current U.S. Terms prohibit using the service to promote other products or services, spam, and unauthorized commercial communications. Rover also tells sitters to report attempts to recruit them for private pet-sitting businesses. A Sitterfolio free-plan-for-feedback offer is product promotion even though it is not a request to take a pet-care booking off-platform.

Use Rover only for broad market understanding. Recruit through independently published business websites or professional social pages, relevant associations or groups whose rules permit research invitations, referrals, and an opt-in Sitterfolio landing page. Do not copy Rover profile text, reviews, or photos into CRM or marketing assets.

Primary sources:

- [Rover U.S. Terms of Service](https://www.rover.com/terms/tos/) — effective August 17, 2026; see sections 4.1 (intended use, competing/promotional use, spam and unauthorized commercial communications), 4.2 (suspension/termination), 7 and 14.1 (content and intellectual-property boundaries).
- [Rover Help: What if someone makes me feel uncomfortable?](https://support.rover.com/hc/en-us/articles/206209920-What-if-someone-makes-me-feel-uncomfortable) — identifies private pet-sitting-business recruitment and pressure to share personal contact information as reportable interactions.
- [Why booking and paying through Rover is important](https://www.rover.com/bookings-on-rover/) — says Rover-originated pet-care arrangements must remain on Rover and warns that off-platform arrangements can lead to account deactivation.

## Safe outreach contract

- Contact only a business channel the sitter independently publishes for business inquiries; never infer an email address or identity from a first name, initial, photo, neighborhood, or username.
- Do not message through Rover or create a fake pet-care inquiry as a pretext.
- State that Sitterfolio is unaffiliated with Rover, participation is optional, and Rover-originated bookings should remain on Rover.
- Describe the consideration precisely: a free Sitterfolio plan for as long as the offered plan exists, in exchange for candid product feedback. Avoid the legally and operationally broader word "forever" unless the product can truly guarantee it.
- Ask for a bounded commitment (for example, a 20-minute onboarding call plus feedback after two weeks), explain what feedback/data will be collected, and provide an easy opt-out.
- Send individually and at low volume. Do not reuse Rover photos, reviews, or biography copy in outreach or Sitterfolio pages without permission.

## Suggested compliant recruitment sources

1. Build a Sitterfolio early-access page with the exact feedback commitment, no credit card, an unaffiliated-with-Rover disclosure, and a short application.
2. Invite opt-ins through the founder's existing pet-care network and participant referrals.
3. Look for independent local pet-sitting businesses with their own websites and explicit business email/contact forms; Rover presence can be disclosed voluntarily by the sitter but should not be the targeting mechanism.
4. Ask moderators before posting in local or professional pet-care communities, and follow each community's promotion/research rules.
5. Consider professional directories or associations that expressly publish member business contact information for discovery; verify the directory's usage terms before outreach.

## Proposed first message (only for a verified independent business channel)

> Hi [first name] — I'm Drew, building Sitterfolio, a simple website and inquiry tool for independent pet sitters. I'm inviting a small group of Chicago-area sitters to use the early plan at no charge for as long as that plan exists, in exchange for a 20-minute setup conversation and candid feedback after two weeks. Sitterfolio isn't affiliated with Rover, and this doesn't change where you handle Rover bookings. Would you be open to seeing the details? If not, just say no and I won't follow up.

Before sending, personalize only with a fact from the sitter's independent business site or other contact-authorized source—not Rover profile content.
